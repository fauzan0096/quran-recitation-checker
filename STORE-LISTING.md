## Full description (max 4000)

```
Bayyin (بيّن) checks your Quran recitation as you read it — on your phone, without sending your voice anywhere.

RECITE • CHECK • IMPROVE
Read an aya, tap Record, and recite. Bayyin hears you on the device itself and colours every word: correct, close, unsure, wrong or missing. You can tell it "✓ sahi tha / ✗ galat tha" so the accuracy takes your own judgement into account.

THE MUSHAF, AS PRINTED
Read the whole mushaf page by page, in Madani lines or IndoPak script, exactly as the text is printed. Quran text is never shortened, changed or reordered.

ROZ KI NAIKI
A daily aya, hadith and sunnah on one calm page.

NAMAZ
Prayer times for your own place (calculated on the phone), qibla compass, and the azaan with your own recording — even while the app is in the background.

DUAEIN • TASBEEH • QIBLA • ISLAMIC CALENDAR
Masnoon duaein with Urdu and Roman transliteration, a tasbeeh with your own target, and the Hijri date.

WHO IT IS FOR
Huffaz, students of hifz and anyone who wants to read the Quran correctly.

FREE
Quran, mushaf, duaein, tasbeeh, qibla, calendar, Roz ki Naiki, khatam plan, inaam and 3 recitation checks a day — free for ever.

PREMIUM
Hifz plan (sabaq / sabqi / murajaa), Hifz Test with the qari on repeat, murajaa reminders and "Meri report" — a small monthly or yearly subscription, bought through Google Play. Cancel any time.

PRIVACY
Your recitation, recordings, tasbeeh and progress stay in your phone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no audio is uploaded. The speech model runs on the device.

The Quran text is the Tanzil Uthmani text and is never altered. Reciter audio is streamed from EveryAyah.com when you ask for it.
```

Urdu full description ke liye alag listing add karna ho to bata dein, tarjuma kar dunga.
