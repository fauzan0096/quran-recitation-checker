Meri yaad-dasht

Har aya par ★ (bookmark) ya ✓ / ↺ ka nishan lagayein — sab kuch isi phone par rehta hai.

Backup

Progress, report aur settings ek file me — phone badalne ya data mitne se pehle save karein.

App install karein

Home screen par icon — bina browser, tez aur offline.

App share karein

Doston ko yeh app bhejein — bas is link se khul jati hai.

Speech model (on-device)

Pehli dafa ~105 MB download hota hai, phir cache se chalta hai.

Default tez hai. "Behtar accuracy" par har check thoda deri lagta hai, natija behtar ho sakta hai.

Qari ki tilawat

    About & privacy

    Bayyin (بيّن) aap ki tilawat ko aya ke Uthmani text se mila kar batata hai kaunsa lafz sahi, qareeb, galat ya chhoot gaya.

    Sab kuch aap ke phone par chalta hai. Na koi server, na account, na audio upload. Mic ka audio sirf usi waqt memory me rehta hai jab tak check na ho jaye.

    App koi personal data collect nahi karti. Settings aur chuni hui surah/aya sirf aap ke browser (local storage) me mehfooz hoti hai.
