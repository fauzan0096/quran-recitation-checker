Privacy policy — Bayyin (بيّن)

Aakhri tabdeeli: 9 November 2026

Chhota jawab: Bayyin koi personal data collect nahi karti. Na account, na hamara koi server, na analytics — aur aap ki tilawat ka audio kabhi upload nahi hota. Poora check aap ke device par hota hai.

Device par kya mehfooz rehta hai

  • Zubaan, night mode, text ka size, chuni hui surah/page — sirf aap ke browser ke local storage me.
  • Recording sirf check hone tak memory me rehti hai, phir mit jati hai. Kisi file me save nahi hoti aur kahin bhejti nahi.
  • IndoPak mushaf ke jo safhat dekh liye, woh cache me reh jate hain taake dobara internet ki zaroorat na ho.

Internet kab istemal hota hai

  • Pehli dafa speech model download hone par (Hugging Face CDN se).
  • IndoPak mushaf ka text Quran.com ke public API se aata hai — request me sirf page number jata hai.
  • In requests me aap ka IP address un services ko nazar aata hai, jaise har website par. Iske ilawa app kahin kuch nahi bhejti.

Microphone

Mic ki ijazat sirf Record dabane par mangi jati hai, aur mic sirf usi waqt chalta hai jab tak recording chal rahi ho.

Subscription / payment

Premium (Hifz plan, Hifz Test, Meri report, be-paana checks) ki payment Google Play leta hai jab app Play Store se install ho. Payment Play ke apne sheet me hoti hai — app ko aap ka card, bank ya email nahi milta; sirf "purchase hui / nahi hui" ka jawab milta hai.

  • Purchase ka record Play account ke saath rehta hai, is app ke server par kuch nahi.
  • Refund aur subscription cancel Play Store se hote hain (Play → Payments & subscriptions).
  • App sirf yeh yaad rakhti hai ke is phone par Premium chalu hai ya nahi.

Jo hum collect nahi karte

  • Naam, email, phone number ya account — kuch bhi nahi.
  • Koi analytics, tracking, ads ya third-party cookie nahi.
  • Koi audio, transcript ya nateeja kisi server par nahi jata.

App delete karne par

App (ya browser ka data) delete karne se zubaan, settings, cache — sab mit jata hai. Kahin aur iski copy nahi rehti.

Bachon ke liye

App har umar ke liye hai aur koi data collect nahi karti, is liye bachon ke liye bhi mehfooz hai.

Tabdeeli

Agar yeh policy badle to yahi page update hoga aur upar ki date badal jayegi. Ahem tabdeeli par app me bhi ek line dikhegi.

Rabta

Koi sawal ho to email karein: gormefauzan@gmail.com — ya GitHub par issue kholen: github.com/fauzan0096/quran-recitation-checker.

English summary

Bayyin collects no personal data. There is no account and no server of ours: the recitation is transcribed and checked entirely on the device and the audio never leaves it. The only network requests are the one-time speech-model download (Hugging Face CDN) and IndoPak mushaf pages (Quran.com public API, page number only). Premium payments are handled by Google Play, so the app never sees a card or an email. Language, theme, reading position and cache live in your browser's local storage; removing the app removes them. No analytics, no ads, no third-party cookies. The microphone is used only while you are recording.
