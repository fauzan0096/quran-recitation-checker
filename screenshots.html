Play Store screenshots — 1080×1920

Phone par jo screen dikhani ho use khol kar screenshot lein (Power + Volume Down), phir yahan daal dein. Ye page har tasveer ko theek 1080×1920 me badal deta hai (bhare hue hisse ko app ke paper rang se pura karta hai). Play Console me yahi PNG upload karein.

Play ke liye ye 6 screen lein (iska order behtar hai)
  1. Quran — aya list, ek aya par ✓/↺ nishan ke saath
  2. Quran — mushaf ka poora page
  3. Tilawat check ka nateeja (lafz rangeen: sahi / kareeb / ghalat)
  4. Roz ki Naiki — aya, hadith, sunnah
  5. Namaz — auqaat aur azaan
  6. Tasbeeh ya Qibla

Tip: phone ka status bar (time, battery) screenshot me aa jata hai — Play usay manzoor karta hai, magar behtar ye hai ke aap pehle "Do not disturb" laga lein aur time theek rakhein.

Bina tick: poori tasveer dikhti hai, khaali jagah paper rang se bharti hai. Tick: poora frame bharta hai, kinaron se thoda kat sakta hai.
